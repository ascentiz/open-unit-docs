# Mechanical Structure: Exo Belt

Exo Belt is the exoskeleton belt and load-bearing mechanical part of the system. This page establishes the formal documentation framework for confirmed mechanical structure, assembly, and fitting information to be added later.

!!! info "Current Status"
    This is a framework page. Except for the confirmed role of Exo Belt as a mechanical system component, all structure names, dimensions, materials, loads, and assembly procedures require confirmation from the mechanical or development team.

## Page Scope

This page will eventually cover:

- Mechanical components and part names
- Mounting relationships among Exo Belt, the Exoskeleton Unit, and B-core Pi 1
- Assembly, disassembly, and inspection procedures
- Fitting, adjustment, and sizing methods
- Permitted loads, range of motion, and mechanical limits
- Routine maintenance, cleaning, storage, and replacement parts
